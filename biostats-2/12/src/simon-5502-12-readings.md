## File details

This readings list was written by Steve Simon on 2026-05-13 and is placed in the public domain.

-   Martha K. Smith. Inappropriately Designating a Factor as Fixed or Random. Part of Common Mistakes in Using Statistics. Available in [html format][ref-smith-nodate].
    -   The context of this page is slightly different (looking at random effects in analysis of variance rather than in hierarchical models or random coefficient models), but you might still find the discussion helpful.
    
-   Mingfeng Xue. A Basic Introduction to Hierarchical Linear Modeling. 2024-03-04. Available in [html format][ref-xue-2024].
    -   A few formulas, but not too technical. A nice introduction to hierarchical models.

[ref-smith-nodate]: https://web.ma.utexas.edu/users/mks/statmistakes/fixedvsrandom.html

[ref-xue-2024]: https://dlab.berkeley.edu/news/basic-introduction-hierarchical-linear-modeling
